    evidence-manifest.md; exempts category-count contexts such as "418
    field/location/standard-clause issues").
  - SP2. No current-state section may claim a total durably-recorded-
    decision count (via the "N decisions are now durably recorded in
    total" phrasing) other than the computed total.
  - SP3. No current-state summary may state that AGC1/VF1 planning-
    agent validation remains pending/required.
  - SP4. Total recorded, per-batch recorded, and pending counts must be
    mutually consistent (batch_h + batch_a == recorded_count).

Design notes: the "historical marker" requirement is sentence-scoped
(not a wide character window) so that an unrelated "historical" mention
elsewhere in a long sentence cannot rescue a genuinely stale, unmarked
count later in that same sentence; the historical-"point" framing
("historical <step> point") is used consistently as the structural
anchor in every corrected historical mention.
